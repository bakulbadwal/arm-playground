    What do the symbols mean?
    t
    The frame you asked for. dataset[i] picks one row of the whole dataset; on this page t is that row's position inside episode 17 (0 … 239).
    offset
    One entry of delta_timestamps, in seconds relative to t. Negative is the past, positive the future, 0.0 is t itself. It has to be a whole number of frames.
    index
    The row that offset turns into: t + round(offset × fps) (the "raw" index), then clamped to the episode's first and last frame (the "used" index). It never crosses into a neighbouring episode.
    is_pad
    One boolean per offset, returned as {feature}_is_pad. True when the raw index fell outside the episode, so the frame you got is a copy of the episode's first or last frame. Loss functions should mask those rows.
    fps
    Frames per second of the recording, 30 for this dataset. 1/fps ≈ 0.0333 s is the finest offset you can request; 0.05 s is 1.5 frames and is rejected.
    batch
    A PyTorch DataLoader stacks B samples along a new first dimension, so every shape gains a leading B (16 here).
